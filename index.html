    Stay detection

    A stay is when someone remains within D metres for at least T minutes. Stays at the same spot become one place. Try the sliders and see places split or disappear.

    About the project

    It was our P-SAT project in fifth year at INSA Lyon, in a team of four, supervised by Antoine Boutet. We used the Priva'Mov dataset: 34.5 million GPS points from 69 volunteers around Lyon, between 2014 and 2016. We kept 10 people over 12 days, about 1.1 million points, found the places where they stop, and wrote 16 questions about them.

    We tested it in the INSA privacy course and showed it to someone from the CNIL (the French data protection authority). They liked it, but told us the real traces could identify the volunteers. In our report we said generated data would fix that, so this version uses six fake people over one week, with the usual problems of a real log: noise, gaps, no signal in the metro and some points a kilometre off.

    Every dot is only a time and a position. None of the answers is written in the data, you find them by looking at where people sleep, where they go during the day and how fast they move.
